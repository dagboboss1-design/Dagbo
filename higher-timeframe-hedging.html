INDEPENDENT ENGINES

H4 & Smart Hedging

Two independent engines. Neither replaces Intraday or Scalping.

← Back to Signal BoardIntraday, Scalping and other profiles remain separate

01 · H4 Higher-Timeframe Engine

Uses closed 4-hour candles for the setup and closed daily candles for directional context. A trade is shown only when the H4 confluence confirms and the daily context does not conflict.

Ready to scan your enabled instruments.

02 · Smart Hedging Engine

Compares enabled FX pairs using synchronized 1H returns, rolling correlation, each leg's independent direction and estimated currency exposure. It will say NO VALID HEDGE when the legs add the same risk. Equal-weight exposure is only a screening estimate, not broker lot sizing.

Ready to analyse correlated FX pairs.

Data source: DAGBO market-data endpoint. Only completed candles are analysed. A missing or stale feed produces DATA UNAVAILABLE, never a guessed signal. Correlation and historical confluence do not guarantee a hedge or profitable trade.
