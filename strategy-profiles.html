Execution rules

    All trading styles

    Trading style is the top-level profile. Indicator combinations, candlestick patterns, chart patterns, price action, SMC/ICT, news, sentiment, macro and risk methods are assigned according to their suitability—not all are forced into every scan.

    The selected profile is shared with the Signal Board. Its enabled strategy categories control which live detector groups are considered: Trend, Momentum, Volatility, Volume, Chart Pattern and Market Structure. The wider strategy library remains a prioritized playbook; a detector is only reported as evidence when the live engine has actually evaluated it from available market data.
