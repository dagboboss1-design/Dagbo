Learn with chart examples

Illustrated examples showing how common price-action concepts look on a chart.

01 · CANDLESTICKS

Bullish and bearish engulfing

A bullish engulfing candle's body covers the previous bearish body. A bearish engulfing is the reverse. Look for a meaningful level and confirmation.

02 · REJECTION

Pin bar / rejection candle

A long lower wick can show bullish rejection; a long upper wick can show bearish rejection. The location of the candle is as important as its shape.

03 · STRUCTURE

Break of Structure (BOS)

A bullish BOS occurs when price closes above a prior swing high. A bearish BOS closes below a prior swing low. A CHoCH is a break against the previous structure and can warn of a shift.

04 · LIQUIDITY

Liquidity sweep

Price briefly moves beyond a visible high or low, then closes back inside the range. A wick beyond the level without a reclaim is not enough confirmation.

05 · IMBALANCE

Fair Value Gap (FVG)

A bullish FVG is the gap between candle one's high and candle three's low when candle three's low is higher. A bearish FVG is the opposite. Price may revisit the zone, but it is not guaranteed to fill.

06 · TRADE PLAN

Entry, Stop Loss and Take Profit

In this BUY example, entry is between the target and stop. The stop belongs beyond the invalidation level. A SELL setup reverses the arrangement. Calculate position size from the stop distance and chosen risk.

Important

These are educational diagrams, not live prices or trade recommendations. Check market hours, broker quotes, spreads and risk before taking any trade.
