How to improve your data

When adding or editing a trade in Journal, select its strategy and timeframe. The Lab will then calculate trade count, win rate, average R and net P&L for each strategy. Older trades without a strategy are grouped as “Not tagged”.

Historical journal results are not proof of future performance. Small samples can be misleading. Include spreads, commissions and slippage when reviewing results.
