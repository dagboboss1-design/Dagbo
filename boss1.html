How to study each setup

1. Context: identify trend, range, higher-timeframe direction, session and nearby liquidity.
2. Confirmation: wait for a close, structure shift, retest, volume or another clear trigger. Do not enter just because a shape appears.
3. Risk: place invalidation where the setup is wrong, calculate position size from stop distance and your risk limit, then plan realistic targets.
4. Review: record screenshots, reason, entry, stop, target and result in your Journal. Back-test before risking real money.

Important price differences

Pip vs point: a pip is a standardised forex price movement unit; a point may mean a full index point, a platform tick or a broker-specific unit. Check the symbol specification.

Spread vs commission: spread is the bid/ask difference; commission is a separate broker charge. Both affect net profit.

Leverage vs margin: leverage controls the exposure available relative to capital; margin is the collateral required to hold that exposure. High leverage magnifies losses as well as gains.

Retracement vs reversal: a retracement is a pullback within a larger move; a reversal is a change in the broader direction and needs structural evidence.

Liquidity sweep vs breakout: a sweep briefly trades beyond a level and may reject; a breakout holds beyond the level and may continue. A candle close and follow-through help distinguish them.

Order block vs FVG: an order block is a price zone identified from a candle/sequence before displacement; a fair value gap is a three-candle imbalance. They are different tools and neither guarantees a reaction.

Education only. Trading leveraged products can cause losses. Practise on demo, verify broker contract specifications and never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
